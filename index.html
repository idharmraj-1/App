Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
How to deposit in Apex Gaming?
Go to Wallet > Click Deposit. You can either deposit USDC directly on the Base network to your dedicated smart contract address (which auto-credits your balance), or select Cash (INR - UPI). For Cash deposits, you pay via UPI and the equivalent USDC is routed to your account.
How does the INR Deposit work?
In the Deposit window, select the Cash option. A dedicated gateway prompt will open where you enter your personal USDC Base wallet address and the USDC amount you want to pay. Payment is only supported via UPI. Once payment is verified, the USDC will be sent directly to your address.
How to claim winnings?
Apex Gaming is built on Web3. After a match is completed, you can claim your winnings directly from the smart contract to your connected USDC (Base) wallet address. Winnings are not sent automatically to prevent network failures.
How to sell crypto without bank freeze?
To prevent bank freeze issues during P2P crypto selling, always transact only with verified merchants with >98% completion rates, verify the payer name matches their registered KYC name, and avoid suspicious transaction remarks.
